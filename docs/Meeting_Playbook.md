# Tazayud Owner PMO — Meeting & Pitch Playbook

**Goal of the meeting:** get agreement to run a **pilot on one portfolio**.
**Not the goal:** explaining every feature. Sell the outcome, demo the proof, ask for the next step.

---

## 1. Before the meeting 

- **Send nothing in advance.** Bring the demo live — surprise beats a forwarded PDF.
- **Open the demo file once** to confirm it runs; leave it on the Corporate Dashboard.
- **Know your room.** Identify the *economic buyer* (signs off), the *champion* (wants it), and the *skeptic* (protects the status quo).
- **Pre-load one real pain** you've heard them mention — use it in the first 60 seconds.

---

## 2. Meeting structure (30 minutes)

| Time | Segment | What you do |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 0–3 min | **Open on their pain** | Name the problem, not the product |
| 3–6 min | **The shift** | Before → After (deck p.4) |
| 6–18 min | **Live demo** | The 6-minute flow — this is the meeting |
| 18–23 min | **ROI + differentiation** | What they gain (deck p.10) |
| 23–28 min | **Objections** | Answer directly (deck p.12) |
| 28–30 min | **The ask** | Pilot on one portfolio (deck p.14) |

---

## 3. The opening (first 3 minutes)

**Don't** start with "Let me show you our system."
**Do** start with their world:

> "Today, controlling billions in development sits across dozens of spreadsheets.
> The dashboard says one thing, the register says another, and no one's sure which is right.
> By month-end, the overrun is already committed.
> What if you could see the truth for any project, at any moment — and prove the numbers are right?"

Then: *"Let me show you."* → go straight to the demo.

---

## 4. Running the live demo (the 6-minute flow)

**This is 60% of the meeting. Slow down. Let them watch.**

1. **Corporate Dashboard** — "Every portfolio, every project, one health view. This is your Monday morning."
2. **Switch scope to Residential** — "Watch everything re-cut." *(Pause. Let the re-render land.)*
3. **Open RES-01 → WBS** — "Drill from the whole company to a single package in two clicks. Here's the one dragging schedule."
4. **AI Document Upload** *(the wow moment — slow right down)*:
   - "Your PMC sends an IPC. Today someone re-types it. Watch this instead."
   - Upload → let the animation run → "It read every field. Confidence scored. I just review and click Create."
   - *Stay silent while it processes. Let them lean in.*
5. **Data Integrity → 10/10** — "Before you ever report a number, the system proves it agrees across every module. Excel can't do this."
6. **AI Assistant + Report** — "Ask it which project needs attention. Now generate the board report. Seconds."

**Demo rule:** after each step, stop talking for 2 seconds. Silence makes them absorb.

> ⚠️ **Keep register drill-downs on RES-01 in this build.** The detailed registers — Cost,
> Variations, Change Log, Procurement, Manpower, Equipment, Quality, HSE, Risk, Issues, WBS —
> currently hold sample data for RES-01 only. If you switch the scope selector to another
> project and open one of those screens, the header and KPIs update but the register table
> below still shows RES-01's rows, and the two sets of totals will not agree.
>
> If someone asks to see a different project: stay at **Corporate or Portfolio scope** and use
> the **Dashboard, Projects, Analytics and Reports** screens — those aggregate correctly across
> all eight projects and are safe at any scope. Say *"register-level detail is loaded for the
> pilot project in this build"* — which is true — and move on. This is fixed in the data-layer
> release.

## 5. Selling points that convert (say these out loud)

- **"Enter once — everything updates."** The single biggest time-saver.
- **"The system proves its own numbers."** The 10/10 integrity engine. No competitor line beats this.
- **"See the truth before the money is spent."** Real-time, not month-end.
- **"Same logic you already trust — finally connected."** Removes the fear of change.
- **"90% less manual data entry."** Concrete, memorable.
- **"Defend every number in the boardroom."** Speaks to the executive's real anxiety.

---

## 6. Reading the room

- **Champion leans in at the demo** → give them more; they'll sell internally for you.
- **Skeptic crosses arms at "AI"** → pivot to integrity + audit trail (control, not magic).
- **Economic buyer checks the time** → jump to ROI and the ask. Don't finish every slide.
- **Someone says "but our Excel works"** → *agree first*: "It does — and this is built from it." (deck p.12)

---

## 7. Objection handling (quick reference)

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Our Excel already works." | "It does — this is built *from* that same logic, with the manual work removed." |
| "Change is disruptive." | "The screens mirror your registers. Learn one, you've learned them all." |
| "Is our data safe?" | "Cloud-secure, role-based, fully audited — safer than files on a shared drive." |
| "Can we start small?" | "Yes — a pilot on one portfolio, on your real projects." |
| "What about our PMC/contractors?" | "Multi-party access with roles — everyone on one governed platform." |
| "How do we trust the numbers?" | "The integrity engine proves them — 10 controls, before you report." |

---

## 8. The close (last 2 minutes)

**Ask for a specific, small next step — not "what do you think?"**

> "Here's what I suggest:
> **One** — we've seen the demo today.
> **Two** — this month, you pick one portfolio.
> **Three** — we run it on your real data, with your team, and prove the value before scaling.
>
> Which portfolio would you want to see it on first?"

That last question assumes the yes and makes them picture it. **Then stop talking.**

---

## 9. After the meeting

- **Leave the demo file** with them — it runs in any browser, no install. Champions will re-open it.
  *(Note: an unattended click-through can hit the RES-01 register limitation above. If you expect
  them to explore alone, say the register detail is loaded for the pilot project in this build.)*
- **Send a 3-line follow-up** within 24 hours: the pilot offer, the one portfolio discussed, a date.
- **Don't send the whole deck cold.** Send it *with* a recap of what they reacted to.

---

## 10. One-line positioning (memorize this)

> **"This is not our Excel workbook online. It's the next-generation Tazayud Owner PMO — built from the intelligence of the controls system you already trust."**
